Here are the best alternatives that handle video well without eating your RAM:

| Browser | ~RAM (10 tabs) | Why it's good for video |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Edge | ~790 MB | **Best pick.** Sleeping Tabs cut memory 32%, full DRM/HTML5 support, plays YouTube/Netflix smoothly. Most optimized for Windows. |
| Firefox | ~700–960 MB | Uses the least RAM at high tab counts (3.8 GB at 50 tabs vs Brave's 4.2). Auto-unloads background tabs when RAM is low. |
| Opera GX | ~899 MB | Has a **hard RAM/CPU limiter** (GX Control) you can cap at 1 GB. Built for gaming + streaming simultaneously. |
| Vivaldi | ~1.4 GB | Auto-hibernates tabs under memory pressure. Good video support, very customizable. |
| Thorium | ~3.1 GB (50 tabs) | 46% less RAM than Chrome, hardware-accelerated video decoding (better for 4K/HEVC). |

**My recommendation for your case:**

- **Edge** if you want the simplest swap — it's already on Windows, uses less RAM than Brave, and the Sleeping Tabs feature is the single most effective trick for keeping memory in check while watching videos.
- **Opera GX** if you want to **hard-cap** memory at a specific limit (e.g. 1 GB) so nothing ever spikes.
- **Firefox** if you want the most RAM-efficient option at high tab counts and don't mind a slightly different UI.

Quick tip regardless of browser: enable **hardware acceleration** (Settings → System) — it offloads video decoding to your GPU and can cut RAM usage by 30–50% on video-heavy tabs.

Here are the best ad blockers ranked by how much memory they **save you** (by blocking heavy ad scripts/iframes) vs. how little they use themselves:

| Ad Blocker | Own RAM Use | RAM Saved Per Tab | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **uBlock Origin** | ~55 MB | ~40 MB per tab | **Best overall.** Lowest footprint, blocks before ads even load. Works on Firefox, Edge, Opera, Brave. |
| **uBlock Origin Lite** | <10 MB (sleeps) | ~20 MB per tab | MV3 version for Chrome/Edge. Lighter but less powerful (no dynamic filtering). |
| **Privacy Badger** | <30 MB | ~10–15 MB | Auto-learns trackers. Great as a **complement** to uBO, not a replacement. |
| **AdGuard** | ~100 MB | ~25 MB | Good YouTube video-ad blocking. Slightly heavier than uBO. |
| **Ghostery** | ~100 MB | ~20 MB | MV3-compatible, decent but not as efficient as uBO. |

**Avoid:** AdBlock Plus and AdBlock (the original) — both use ~200 MB of RAM themselves, which defeats the purpose.

### My recommendation

**uBlock Origin** is the clear winner. It's the only one that *nets positive* — it uses ~55 MB but saves ~40 MB **per tab** by preventing ad iframes and scripts from loading. With 10 tabs open, that's roughly 300–600 MB of RAM you're not wasting on ads.

Pair it with **Privacy Badger** (~25 MB) for extra tracker protection if you want.

**Bonus:** If you stay on Brave, note that they overhauled their built-in adblock engine in January 2026, cutting its own memory by **75%** (~45 MB saved). So Brave's built-in Shields are lighter than before — but uBlock Origin on Firefox or Edge will still be your best overall combo for memory.
