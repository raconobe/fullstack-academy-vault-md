### 🆚 Obsidian vs. Cloud Alternatives: Key Trade-offs

#### 1. **Data Ownership & Format**

- **Obsidian & Logseq:** Save pure `.md` (Markdown) files locally on your hard drive. Your data is never trapped in a proprietary database.
    
      
    
- **Notion:** Stores notes in a proprietary relational database in the cloud. If Notion goes down or changes pricing, exporting complex databases can be tricky.
    
      
    

#### 2. **Free Cloud Syncing for Obsidian**

Because Obsidian uses standard folders and `.md` files, you can get **100% free cloud backup & multi-device sync** without paying for Obsidian Sync:

  

- **Option A: Git / GitHub (Popular for Developers)**
    
      
    
    Use the official free `obsidian-git` community plugin to automatically commit and push your vault to a private GitHub repository.
    
      
    
- **Option B: Syncthing / Cloud Drives**
    
      
    
    Keep your Obsidian vault folder inside Google Drive, Nextcloud, or Dropbox, or sync it directly between your Linux laptop and mobile device using **Syncthing**.
    
      
    

### 💡 Recommendation

- If you want a **zero-setup cloud experience** with rich databases and formatting out of the box, **Notion** is the easiest free-tier option.
    
      
    
- If you prefer **Markdown control, speed, and privacy**, sticking with **Obsidian** (and setting up a free Git repo or Syncthing) will give you total ownership over your course notes without cloud storage caps.
